# Linked Lists — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Reverse Linked List** — iterative and recursive.
2. **Merge Two Sorted Lists** — splice into a single sorted list.
3. **Linked List Cycle** — detect a cycle (Floyd's tortoise and hare).

## Medium

1. **Add Two Numbers** — digit-by-digit sum of two number-lists.
2. **Remove Nth Node From End of List** — single-pass two-pointer.
3. **Reorder List** — `L0 → Ln → L1 → Ln−1 → ...`.

## Hard

1. **Reverse Nodes in k-Group** — reverse every `k` consecutive nodes.
2. **Merge k Sorted Lists** — heap or divide-and-conquer merge.
3. **LRU Cache** — `O(1)` `get`/`put` (hashmap + doubly linked list).
