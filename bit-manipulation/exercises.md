# Bit Manipulation — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Single Number** — the one element that appears once (others appear twice).
2. **Number of 1 Bits** — population count (Hamming weight).
3. **Counting Bits** — for `i` in `[0, n]`, return `popcount(i)`.

## Medium

1. **Sum of Two Integers** — add without `+` or `−`.
2. **Reverse Bits** — reverse the 32 bits of an unsigned integer.
3. **Bitwise AND of Numbers Range** — AND of all integers in `[m, n]`.

## Hard

1. **Maximum XOR of Two Numbers in an Array** — best pair via a binary trie.
2. **Single Number III** — two unique elements among duplicates.
3. **Concatenation of Consecutive Binary Numbers** — value of `"1" + "10" + ... + "n"` mod 1e9+7.
