# Stacks and Queues — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Valid Parentheses** — match `()`, `[]`, `{}` with a stack.
2. **Implement Queue using Stacks** — two-stack queue.
3. **Min Stack** — `push`, `pop`, `top`, and `getMin` in `O(1)`.

## Medium

1. **Daily Temperatures** — days until a warmer temperature (monotonic stack).
2. **Evaluate Reverse Polish Notation** — postfix expression evaluation.
3. **Decode String** — expand `k[encoded_string]` patterns.

## Hard

1. **Largest Rectangle in Histogram** — `O(n)` via monotonic stack.
2. **Trapping Rain Water** — monotonic stack variant.
3. **Basic Calculator** — parse and evaluate `+`, `−`, parentheses, and unary signs.
