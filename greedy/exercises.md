# Greedy — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Assign Cookies** — pair children with cookies to maximize satisfied count.
2. **Lemonade Change** — can you give change with `$5`/`$10`/`$20` bills?
3. **Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock II** — max profit with unlimited transactions.

## Medium

1. **Jump Game** — can you reach the last index from the first?
2. **Gas Station** — starting station that lets you complete the circuit.
3. **Task Scheduler** — min CPU intervals with cooldown `n`.

## Hard

1. **Candy** — minimum candies satisfying neighbor constraints.
2. **Jump Game II** — minimum number of jumps to reach the end.
3. **Minimum Number of Refueling Stops** — fewest stops to reach the target distance.
