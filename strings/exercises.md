# Strings — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Valid Palindrome** — ignoring case and non-alphanumeric characters.
2. **Reverse String** — in-place reversal.
3. **Implement strStr() / indexOf** — first occurrence of a needle.

## Medium

1. **Longest Palindromic Substring** — expand-around-center or Manacher.
2. **String to Integer (atoi)** — robust string-to-int parsing.
3. **Group Anagrams** — group strings sharing the same letter histogram.

## Hard

1. **Regular Expression Matching** — `.` and `*` matching (DP).
2. **Minimum Window Substring** — smallest substring of `s` covering `t`.
3. **Shortest Palindrome** — shortest palindrome by prepending characters (KMP).
