# Hashmaps — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Valid Anagram** — are two strings anagrams of each other?
2. **Two Sum** — pair of indices summing to a target.
3. **First Unique Character in a String** — index of the first non-repeating character.

## Medium

1. **Group Anagrams** — bucket strings by their sorted-letter key.
2. **Top K Frequent Elements** — `k` most frequent values (bucket sort or heap).
3. **Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters** — sliding window with a last-seen map.

## Hard

1. **Substring with Concatenation of All Words** — start indices of concatenated word permutations.
2. **Longest Consecutive Sequence** — longest run of consecutive ints in `O(n)`.
3. **LFU Cache** — `O(1)` `get`/`put` with frequency eviction.
