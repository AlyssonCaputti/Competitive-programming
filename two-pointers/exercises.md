# Two Pointers — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Valid Palindrome** — converging pointers ignoring non-alphanumerics.
2. **Move Zeroes** — move all zeros to the end, preserving order.
3. **Two Sum II — Input Array Is Sorted** — converging pointers on a sorted array.

## Medium

1. **3Sum** — sort + two-pointer scan to skip duplicates.
2. **Container With Most Water** — converge from the ends.
3. **Remove Duplicates from Sorted Array II** — keep at most two of each value in-place.

## Hard

1. **Trapping Rain Water** — two pointers tracking left/right max.
2. **4Sum** — generalized k-Sum.
3. **Subarrays with K Different Integers** — exactly-`k` reduces to at-most-`k − 1`.
