# Arrays — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Two Sum** — return the indices of the two numbers that add up to a target.
2. **Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock** — maximum profit from a single buy/sell.
3. **Contains Duplicate** — check whether any value appears at least twice.

## Medium

1. **Product of Array Except Self** — for each `i`, product of all elements except `nums[i]`, no division.
2. **Maximum Subarray** — largest contiguous subarray sum (Kadane).
3. **3Sum** — all unique triplets summing to zero.

## Hard

1. **First Missing Positive** — smallest missing positive integer in `O(n)` time and `O(1)` extra space.
2. **Trapping Rain Water** — total water trapped between bars of varying heights.
3. **Median of Two Sorted Arrays** — median in `O(log(min(m, n)))`.
