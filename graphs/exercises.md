# Graphs — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Find Center of Star Graph** — identify the central node.
2. **Find if Path Exists in Graph** — undirected reachability between two nodes.
3. **Find the Town Judge** — in-degree `n − 1`, out-degree `0`.

## Medium

1. **Number of Islands** — count connected components in a 2D grid.
2. **Course Schedule** — detect a cycle in a directed graph (topological sort).
3. **Clone Graph** — deep-copy an undirected graph.

## Hard

1. **Word Ladder** — shortest transformation length (BFS).
2. **Alien Dictionary** — recover an ordering from sorted words (toposort).
3. **Critical Connections in a Network** — find bridges (Tarjan).
