# Math — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Palindrome Number** — without converting to a string.
2. **Fizz Buzz** — classic divisibility output.
3. **Plus One** — add one to a digit-array number.

## Medium

1. **Pow(x, n)** — fast exponentiation in `O(log n)`.
2. **Excel Sheet Column Title** — base-26 conversion (1-indexed).
3. **Integer to Roman** — greedy conversion using value/symbol pairs.

## Hard

1. **Count Primes** — Sieve of Eratosthenes up to `n`.
2. **Permutation Sequence** — the `k`-th permutation of `[1..n]` without enumeration.
3. **Max Points on a Line** — most colinear points (slope hashing).
