# Sliding Window — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Maximum Average Subarray I** — max average over a window of size `k`.
2. **Contains Duplicate II** — any two equal values within `k` indices?
3. **Substrings of Size Three with Distinct Characters** — count good substrings.

## Medium

1. **Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters** — variable-size window.
2. **Permutation in String** — does `s2` contain any permutation of `s1`?
3. **Fruit Into Baskets** — longest subarray with at most two distinct values.

## Hard

1. **Minimum Window Substring** — smallest window of `s` covering all chars of `t`.
2. **Sliding Window Maximum** — max of each `k`-window via monotonic deque.
3. **Substring with Concatenation of All Words** — starts of fixed-length concatenations.
