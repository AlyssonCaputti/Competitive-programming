# Sorting — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Sort Array By Parity** — even numbers before odd ones.
2. **Squares of a Sorted Array** — sorted squares in `O(n)` (two pointers).
3. **Merge Sorted Array** — merge `nums2` into `nums1` in-place.

## Medium

1. **Sort Colors** — Dutch National Flag (three-way partition).
2. **Kth Largest Element in an Array** — quickselect (`O(n)` average).
3. **Sort Characters by Frequency** — bucket sort by character count.

## Hard

1. **Merge Intervals** — merge overlapping intervals after sorting.
2. **Maximum Gap** — largest gap between sorted elements in linear time (radix or bucket).
3. **Count of Smaller Numbers After Self** — merge-sort with index tracking or BIT.
