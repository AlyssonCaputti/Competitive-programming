# Backtracking — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Subsets** — all possible subsets of a set of distinct integers.
2. **Generate Parentheses** — all well-formed combinations of `n` pairs.
3. **Letter Combinations of a Phone Number** — string-to-digit combinations.

## Medium

1. **Permutations** — all permutations of a list of distinct integers.
2. **Combination Sum** — combinations that sum to a target (reuse allowed).
3. **Word Search** — does the word exist on the grid (4-directional)?

## Hard

1. **N-Queens** — place `n` non-attacking queens on an `n × n` board.
2. **Sudoku Solver** — fill a partially-completed 9×9 board.
3. **Word Search II** — find all dictionary words on the grid (Trie + DFS).
