# Recursion — Exercises

Solutions go in [`python/`](python/) and [`cpp/`](cpp/).

## Easy

1. **Fibonacci Number** — naive recursion + memoization.
2. **Power of Two** — `n > 0` and `n & (n − 1) == 0`, or recursive halving.
3. **Reverse String** — in-place via recursion.

## Medium

1. **Pow(x, n)** — recursive fast power.
2. **K-th Symbol in Grammar** — derive bit at `(n, k)` recursively.
3. **Permutations** — enumerate all orderings.

## Hard

1. **Regular Expression Matching** — recursive `.` / `*` matching.
2. **Wildcard Matching** — `?` and `*` against a pattern.
3. **Strobogrammatic Number II** — generate all `n`-digit strobogrammatic numbers.
